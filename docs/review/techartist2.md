# Technical Artist 2 report

Method: read docs/MARKET.md, src/music.js, src/extras.js, all five extras-config.js songs, all five Sfx implementations, the five main.js wiring paths, src/box.js (iframe swap), src/games.js (mute keys), ios/GameBox/*.swift. Rendered every song with a Node script (scratchpad/render.mjs) using Music.notesFor over 8 bars (note grid, kick/hat, chord quality, lead range, non-chord-tone ratio, loop check) and computed mix levels in dBFS from the gain chain.

## Scores (1–10) per game: "music quality", "sfx quality", "mix"
- MELT — music 6, sfx 7, mix 5. A pentatonic-minor Asus–Esus–Am–C is floaty and safe; phase-change signatures are the best-designed SFX in the box; heat bed is dead code.
- SKIP — music 7, sfx 8, mix 6. D–Bm–G–A at 72 bpm, no drums, sine lead with 0.9 s decay: the one song that fits its game. Water/cricket beds + pentatonic streak chimes are excellent.
- POP — music 5, sfx 6, mix 4. C–F–G–Am is fine, but a square-wave lead at 440–988 Hz firing 73/128 steps sits exactly on top of the pop SFX register.
- ORBIT — music 5, sfx 4, mix 4. Em–C–Am–D is a good progression; saw bass at E2 (82 Hz) is inaudible on a phone, and the one cue that saves a run (warn) is the quietest sound in the game.
- SKY TEMPLE — music 5, sfx 7, mix 5. Bar 3 is a diminished triad in a "calm temple" stacker; sine bass at G2 (98 Hz) vanishes on speakers; straight-8th hats at 100 bpm read as a metronome.

## Findings (ordered by impact; max 12)
- [P0] Drums are sent through the dotted-8th feedback delay and nothing limits the master: `this.out` collects all four busses and feeds both `master` and `delay` (feedback 0.3–0.5, 380–450 ms), so every kick echoes 3–4 times and smears the groove; kick peaks at −11 dBFS while the lead sits at −25..−31 dBFS, and summed peaks in Orbit play mode reach ~0.5 + 0.35 wet with no compressor — src/music.js `attach()`/`playStep()` — connect `busses.drums` straight to `master` (not `out`); insert `DynamicsCompressorNode` (threshold −18 dB, knee 12, ratio 4, attack 3 ms, release 150 ms) then a master `lowpass` at 6.5 kHz before `destination`; `drums.vol` 0.45–0.55 → 0.30 in all five extras-config.js.
- [P0] Music masks gameplay SFX and there is no ducking: music master is 0.55 (−5 dBFS) with kick at −11 dB; SFX land at −15 dBFS (Sky perfect) down to −32 dBFS (Orbit warn), so the thing the player must hear is 6–20 dB under the bed — src/music.js, src/extras.js, each Sfx — set `Music.volume` 0.55 → 0.35 (−9 dB), raise SFX masters 0.5 → 0.7, add `Music.duck(db = −6, hold = 120 ms, release = 250 ms)` using `master.gain.setTargetAtTime`, expose it via `extras.music`, and call it from every Sfx event that matters (pop/perfect/skip/phase/gem/wrong/crash/miss).
- [P0] iOS interruption/background never recovers until the next tap: every `unlock()` only resumes when `ctx.state === 'suspended'`, but WebKit reports `'interrupted'` after a call, Siri, Control Center, or AirPods switch; no `visibilitychange` or `onstatechange` calls `resume()`, and `Music.start()` just mutes `out` while hidden. On a title screen that means silence until a tap; mid-run the first tap after returning is a dead gesture — melt/src/audio/sfx.js, skip/src/audio/sfx.js, sky-temple/src/audio/sfx.js, pop/src/main.js and orbit/src/main.js `Sfx.unlock`, src/music.js `start()` — change the guard to `if (ctx.state !== 'running') ctx.resume()`, add `ctx.onstatechange` and `document.addEventListener('visibilitychange', () => !document.hidden && ctx.resume())`, and also call `unlock()` from a `touchend`/`click` listener because `pointerdown` is not a reliable user activation on older iOS Safari (canvas `press()` only uses pointerdown).
- [P1] The iOS shell never configures AVAudioSession: default SoloAmbient stops the player's Spotify/podcast on launch and makes the first sound a jarring takeover — ios/GameBox/GameBoxApp.swift `didFinishLaunching` — `try AVAudioSession.sharedInstance().setCategory(.ambient, mode: .default, options: [.mixWithOthers]); setActive(true)`; inject `window.__gameBoxNative.otherAudio = isOtherAudioPlaying` in GameView.swift's user script and in src/extras.js `unlock()` skip `music.start()` (keep SFX) when it is true.
- [P1] Bass fundamentals are below phone-speaker range: Orbit saw at E2 (82 Hz), Sky Temple sine at G2 (98 Hz), Melt triangle at A2 (110 Hz); a sine at 98 Hz is effectively inaudible on an iPhone speaker (roll-off ~300 Hz), so three games have no low end at all — src/music.js `playStep()` plus sky-temple/orbit extras-config.js — add a second bass voice one octave up at `vol * 0.5`, lowpass 1200 Hz, 10 ms attack (gives audible harmonics without boom); set Sky Temple `bass.type` sine → triangle; Orbit `bass.vol` 0.12 → 0.10 with lowpass 700 Hz on the saw.
- [P1] Sky Temple bar 3 is a diminished triad: `[3,5,0]` in G lydian = C#–E–G (#IV°), the bass drops to C#3 while the lead stays G-centred, and it recurs every 9.6 s — sky-temple/src/extras-config.js — progression → `[[0,2,4],[1,3,5],[4,6,1],[2,4,6]]` (G – A – D – Bm, bright and resolving); hats → only on `pos % 4 === 2` (offbeats) at `drums.vol` 0.2 so 100 bpm stops ticking like a metronome.
- [P1] Pop's lead is a square wave at 440–988 Hz, 160 ms, lowpass 5 kHz, firing on 73 of 128 steps with 21 non-chord tones (e.g. F5 over Am, B5 over G) and random leaps (A4→G5→B4); it buzzes in exactly the band of the pop() SFX (520–1560 Hz) — pop/src/extras-config.js, src/music.js `notesFor()` — `lead: { type: 'triangle', vol: 0.06, density: 0.5, octave: 1, decay: 0.2 }` with lead cutoff 2500 Hz; in `notesFor` track `lastLead` and 70% of the time pick the chord tone nearest the previous note (±1 scale step), restricting the non-chord branch to passing tones on odd steps only.
- [P1] Orbit SFX priority is inverted: `warn()` (arc going solid, the cue that saves a run) is a 180 Hz square at vol 0.05 for 80 ms (−32 dBFS) and vanishes under the kick; `crash()` is an unfiltered sawtooth 300→60 Hz at 0.3 — the harshest sound in the box, played at every death. Pop `wrong()` and Sky Temple `miss()` are also raw sawtooth — orbit/src/main.js `Sfx`, pop/src/main.js `Sfx`, sky-temple/src/audio/sfx.js — warn → triangle 740→880 Hz 60 ms vol 0.22 plus a 1.2 kHz 15 ms tick; crash/miss/wrong → triangle 220→55 Hz 0.45 s vol 0.25 through a 1500 Hz lowpass plus 600 Hz lowpass noise 0.3 s; add a `cutoff` lowpass stage to the generic `tone()` in every Sfx (default 4000 Hz).
- [P2] Loops are obvious: harmony repeats every 4 bars (Pop 7.5 s, Melt 8.6 s, Orbit 8.1 s, Sky 9.6 s, Skip 13.3 s); bass pattern and hats are identical every bar; kick varies only by a 40% hit on step 10; only the lead is non-repeating. Pad voicings also have no voice-leading (each chord re-spelled inside root..root+11) — src/music.js `notesFor()` and the five extras-config.js — supply 8-chord progressions (the engine already supports any length: e.g. Melt `[[0,2,4],[3,0,2],[1,3,0],[4,1,3],[0,2,4],[2,4,1],[3,0,2],[4,1,3]]`), add optional `bass.patternB` used on odd bars, drop the hat on step 14 of `bar % 4 === 3` and add kick on 12 and 14 on `bar % 8 === 7` as a fill, and pick the pad inversion closest to the previous pad (minimise summed semitone movement).
- [P2] Melt's heat bed is dead code and Melt's hold has no sound: `startHeatBed()` creates a permanently looping BufferSource and `setHeat()` has zero callers in melt/src — melt/src/audio/sfx.js, melt/src/main.js — either drive `setHeat(0..1)` from the tap build-up (ramp over the 2-tap transitions) or delete the bed; while there, `setMuted` in all Sfx sets gain instantly (click) — use `setTargetAtTime(v, t, 0.03)`.
- [P2] Missing SFX moments: no "new best" fanfare in any of the five (the newBest badge appears silently), no music sting on game over (`setMode('over')` only thins), Pop and Orbit milestones have a text pop but no sound (Melt/Skip/Sky have `milestone()`), no revive/continue whoosh, Orbit's ring accelerates while the song stays at 118 bpm, and the hub's `frame.src = 'about:blank'` cuts audio dead — pop/src/main.js, orbit/src/main.js, src/extras.js `onOver`, src/box.js `close()` — add `newBest()` (pentatonic 4-note rise, triangle, 0.5 s, vol 0.16) gated on `isBest`, a 2-note pad resolve (5th→root, 1.2 s) in `onOver`, `milestone()` to Pop/Orbit, in Orbit set `music.song.bpm = 118 + 22 * speedNorm` (stepLen is recomputed each `schedule()`; also update `delay.delayTime`), and fade `music.master` to 0 over 300 ms before swapping the iframe src.
- [P2] Hats are 40 ms white noise highpassed at 7 kHz on every 8th note, pure sibilance on a phone speaker, and a new buffer is allocated per hit (GC churn at 128 bpm). Swing (`swing: 0.12` in Pop) only shifts odd 16ths so hats never swing — src/music.js `hat()` — one shared 50 ms noise buffer, bandpass 6 kHz Q 1.5, 25 ms, accent levels 0.3/0.18, and apply swing to hats by placing the accent hat on odd steps when `swing > 0`.
