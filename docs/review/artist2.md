- [P1] The MELT droplet is flat (one fill, one edge, one highlight) and floats with no contact shadow, yet altitude is the hidden variable that decides spikes (≥0.6) and beams (≤0.4). — melt / `renderer.js` `drawPlayer` — Radial body gradient centred (−0.3R, −0.35R), r 0.1R→R: water `#8fd3ff`/`#3aa7ff`/`#1b6fc4` at 0/0.55/1; ice `#e6fbff`/`#bdf0ff`/`#7fc9e8`; steam `#ffffff`/`#f6f8ff`/`#cfd7ec`; 1.5px rim light `rgba(255,255,255,0.35)` over the bottom arc 0.15π–0.85π.
