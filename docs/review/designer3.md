## Findings (ordered by impact; max 12)
- [P0] Tutorial is a pre-game slideshow in all five games, exactly what the brief forbids; it alone costs 3 of the 5 taps — `src/extras.js` `showTutorial()/beforeStart()`, `skip/src/main.js` `firstPlay()`, `*/src/extras-config.js` — Delete the slideshow. First run starts on tap and shows one coach mark at the moment it matters, then never again: MELT "TAP → become STEAM" as the first spike wall is 1.5 s away (scroll at 60% speed until the first tap); SKIP already has the right hints, just drop its slides; POP an arrow + "Tap the GRAPE ones" on the first matching bubble; ORBIT "TAP to reverse" when the first arc flashes; Sky Temple "TAP to drop" as the stone first crosses the tower. Keep `?` → a How-to sheet for replay. Target: launch → playing in 2 taps.
- [P0] In-run score is drawn under the hub's GAMES pill in MELT and Sky Temple (screenshots `*-06-melt-playing`, `*-23-sky-temple-mid-run`) — `melt/index.html`, `sky-temple/index.html` have no `.hud-top`, so `.in-box #hud .hud-top{margin-top:6vh}` in `css/game-ui.css:70` never applies — Wrap the score in `<div class="hud-top">` in both files, or change the rule to `.in-box #hud{padding-top:calc(var(--safe-top) + 56px)}`.
- [P0] POP is unplayable with colour-vision deficiency: bubbles differ by hue only; under deuteranopia/protanopia GRAPE (target) and SKY both read blue, MINT and ROSE both grey (`*-23-pop-cb-deutan.png`, `*-cb-protan.png`); the iridescent rim adds unrelated hues — `pop/src/render/renderer.js` `bubble()`, `drawTarget()`, `pop/src/main.js hud()` — Give each palette entry a glyph drawn inside the bubble and in the HUD target (ROSE ♥, SKY ▲, MINT ●, SUN ★, GRAPE ◆), set the rim to the bubble's own hue, HUD copy "POP ◆ GRAPE". Ship it on by default; no setting needed.
