## Findings (ordered by severity; max 12)

- [P0] SKIP's timing judgement does not reward humans: a tap after contact is never credited, so a player who literally follows the hint "TAP WHEN IT TOUCHES THE WATER" scores like someone who never taps — `/home/user/Mythic-World/skip/src/core/skip.js:126-135` (`skipTap` only accepts `ttc <= EARLY_WINDOW`; `contact()` at :184-191 only judges a tap that preceded contact) and `/home/user/Mythic-World/skip/src/main.js:305` — Sim, one tap per hop, 40 seeds: never tap = 41 m; human N(+40 ms late, σ 60 ms) = median 44 m; human N(0, σ 40 ms) = median 63 m; frame-perfect autopilot = 30,476 m. The reward curve is a cliff between "perfect" (streak runaway) and "anything else" (decay), and an untapped idle throw's hops shrink 0.68 → 0.38 → 0.33 → 0.28 s so the run is over in 4.1 s with ~3 hops a novice could even time. Fix: (1) symmetric window: record `lastContactT`/`vxBefore` in `contact()`, and in `skipTap()` if `time - lastContactT <= LATE_WINDOW (0.10)` re-judge the last skip retroactively (set `vx = vxBefore*retain(q)+boost`, `vy = bounce(q)`); (2) `PERFECT_WINDOW 0.11 → 0.14`, `GOOD_WINDOW 0.24 → 0.28`; (3) novice decay: `RETAIN_BASE 0.86 → 0.90`, `BOUNCE_MIN_QUALITY 0.70 → 0.85` (sim: untapped run 4.1 → 5.7 s, hops ≥0.3 s go 3 → 6); (4) change the hint to "TAP AS THE REFLECTION MEETS IT".
- [P1] POP's target switch kills the player: 75 of 77 life-losses by a competent human-sim (0.3 s reaction) happened within 1.2 s of a target change, because bubbles of the new colour already near the top escape before anyone can react — `/home/user/Mythic-World/pop/src/core/pop.js:109-114` (`changeTarget` recolours nothing) and `:67-71` (escape penalty has no grace) — 112/300 switches had a new-target bubble already in the top 40 % (< 2.5 s from the edge). Fix: in `changeTarget()` recolour new-target bubbles with `b.y < WORLD_H*0.5` to `from`, and set `this.graceUntil = time + 0.8`; in `update()` skip `loseLife('escape')` while `time < graceUntil`. Add a test: "no escape life-loss within 1 s of a target change on 50 seeds".
- [P1] ORBIT's warning is shorter than the travel time, so the wall closes on the comet: 780/782 blockers (50 seeds × 60 s) are reached before `BLOCK_WARN + 0.25 s`; spawn-to-edge time is 0.45–1.19 s (median 0.73) against a 0.9 s warn, and the first one spawns 1.2 s into the run — `/home/user/Mythic-World/orbit/src/core/orbit.js:16-18,91-95,101-103`, warn SFX plays only at solidify `/home/user/Mythic-World/orbit/src/main.js:42` — Fix: rule "a wall never closes on you": in `update()` when `b.age >= BLOCK_WARN` and the comet overlaps `b`, keep it `warn` until the comet exits, then emit `solid` (dashing through becomes a legitimate near-miss: pop "THROUGH! +2"); `BLOCK_WARN 0.9 → 1.2`; first `blockIn 1.2 → 2.5`; play `sfx.warn()` + `haptics.light()` on the `block` event, not `solid`.
- [P1] SKIP tap-mashing earns perfect streaks and perfect play has no speed ceiling: each new tap overwrites the pending one (`skip.js:130-131`), so tapping every 50 ms produced a 14-perfect streak and 106 m with zero skill; a real perfect player hits vx 52 m/s after 40 skips (31 m hops) and 19,000 m in 60 s, making the 300 m "far shore" milestone, the leaderboard and challenge scores meaningless — `/home/user/Mythic-World/skip/src/core/skip.js:130-131,198-200` — Fix: one tap per hop (`if (this.tapLead !== null) { this.spoiled = true; }` → quality 0 at contact); `s.vx = Math.min(c.VX_MAX, …)` with `VX_MAX 26`; `PERFECT_BOOST_CAP 2.0 → 1.2`. Keep the dip only for genuinely early taps: `skip.js:133` change `ttc > c.PERFECT_WINDOW` to `ttc > c.GOOD_WINDOW` (today a "good" tap 0.2 s early is kicked to vy −7 and loses 1.1 m of hop).
- [P1] MELT's seeded course depends on screen width, so "Same run, same luck" is false between phone and iPad/desktop: `spawnX` comes from the viewport (`/home/user/Mythic-World/melt/src/core/melt.js:63-64,177`) and `fillAhead` sizes every gap and hazard roll from the live `score` at spawn time (`:120,127-130`), so wider views generate earlier at lower score — seed 11 at 390 px vs 820 px: `glass6 pipe4 geyser3 vent2` vs `glass7 geyser2 pipe1`. Fix: generate from a device-independent counter (`this.spawned`) instead of `this.score` for `difficulty()`, `speedForScore()` and the hazard roll, and fill to a fixed `GEN_AHEAD = 1000` world px regardless of view; add a test asserting identical obstacle sequences for `setViewWidth(390)` vs `(1024)`.
- [P1] MELT drops the second tap of a fast double-tap: `TAP_COOLDOWN 0.08` discards any tap < 80 ms after the last (`melt.js:82`; config `:14`), and two-tap transitions (water→ice for glass, ice→steam for spikes) are routine — sim: double-tap 70 ms apart → second tap returns `null`, player dies as steam at a glass wall. Fix: replace the drop with a 1-deep buffer (`this.queued = true` → applied in `update()` once `time - lastTap >= TAP_COOLDOWN`), cooldown 0.06; update the "taps are debounced" test to assert the tap is deferred, not lost.
- [P2] ORBIT's escalation is invisible inside a run: speed cap needs 125 gems (`SPEED_RAMP 0.012`), autopilot median is 22 gems at 30 s / 43 at 60 s, and reversing has no score meaning — `/home/user/Mythic-World/orbit/src/core/orbit.js:11-13,19-21,104-108` — Fix: `SPEED_RAMP 0.03` (cap at 50 gems), `BLOCK_GAP_RAMP 0.05`, `MAX_BLOCKS 3` from score 30; add a gem chain: a gem collected within 1.5 s of the last raises `chain` (×2…×5) and scores `chain` points with a growing pop + haptic, chain resets on timeout.
- [P2] POP's combo is decorative and rise speed is uncapped: `score++` only (`pop.js:89`), while `speedFor()` (`:46`) keeps ramping to 390–430 px/s at score 200 (1.6 s to cross) — Fix: `score += 1 + Math.min(3, Math.floor(combo/5))`; at `combo === 10` fire a 3 s "FRENZY" (every on-screen bubble becomes the target colour, then the target switches) for the chain-reaction moment the brief asks for; add `SPEED_CAP 190` applied in `speedFor()`.
- [P2] Sky Temple's perfect window shrinks to 39 ms at max speed and nothing tests human timing: `PERFECT_TOLERANCE 0.085` is absolute (`/home/user/Mythic-World/sky-temple/src/core/config.js:21`, used at `stack.js:204`) so the window is 89 ms at score 0, 50 ms at 50, 39 ms at 83; a σ 50 ms human-sim medians 26 (min 14), σ 80 ms medians 14 — `/home/user/Mythic-World/sky-temple/tests/stack.test.js` only drops at exact offsets — Fix: `tolerance = max(0.085, speed * 0.035)` (keeps ≥ 70 ms), `MAX_SPEED 4.4 → 3.8`; compensate display latency in `drop()` by rewinding `m[axis] -= dir*speed*0.035`; add a test "σ=50 ms jittered drops reach ≥ 15 on 20 seeds"; add a combo-5/10 screen-wide flash + `score += combo` bonus so the ×21 streak in the screenshot is celebrated, not just labelled.
- [P2] MELT's first run asks for a tap at 1.93 s with no in-run coaching half the time: `chooseNext(null)` picks spikes (needs steam) in 99/200 seeds for the very first obstacle (`melt.js:105-109,121`) — Fix: force `beam` for `spawned < 2` (water passes with zero taps → failure-free first ~4 s), then spikes with the existing `FLOAT` hint; also give close calls a value (`closeCalls` are counted at `:165-166` but score nothing): close-call streak of 3 → `+3` and a "HOT STREAK" pop.
- [P2] Challenge replays are not the same run in POP/ORBIT and SKIP is frame-rate dependent: POP draws the target RNG on the player's 10th pop (`pop.js:111`) and ORBIT's `spawnGem` consumes 1–8 draws depending on comet angle (`orbit.js:76-82`), so the sequence diverges from the challenger's after the first difference; SKIP integrates with variable `dt` (`skip.js:150-158`, 60 vs 120 Hz differ) — Fix: two seeded streams per game (`rngSpawn = seededRandom(seed)`, `rngPlayer = seededRandom(seed ^ 0x9E3779B9)`) and a fixed-step accumulator (1/120 s) inside `SkipGame.update`; soften the copy at `/home/user/Mythic-World/src/extras.js:71` ("Same run, same luck") until then.
- [P2] Fairness tests use oracle autopilots, not humans: SKIP (`skip/tests/skip.test.js:140-152`) and POP (`pop/tests/pop.test.js:43-48`) tap with zero reaction and frame-perfect timing, ORBIT reacts instantly to spawns; only MELT (`melt/tests/melt.test.js:66-79`) models a 0.25 s delay — Fix: add a shared `humanPolicy(reaction 0.25 s, jitter σ 50 ms)` helper under `/home/user/Mythic-World/tests/` and assert per game: SKIP ≥ 80 m median, POP survives 60 s with ≤ 1 escape death, ORBIT 45 s on 10 seeds, Sky Temple ≥ 15 — these would have caught the P0/P1 above.
