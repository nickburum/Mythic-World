# Game Box — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Game Box is a collection of five small games. It is free and works offline.

**What we collect.** Game Box itself does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. Your best scores are kept on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud Key-Value Storage, which only you can access.

**Game Center.** If you are signed into Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center alias. Apple's privacy policy governs this data.

**Ads.** After a run ends you may choose to watch a rewarded video to continue. There are no banners and nothing plays unless you tap it. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Game Box never asks to track you and never shares an advertising identifier, so the ads are not personalised; AdMob still receives basic technical data (such as IP address and device type) to deliver and measure an ad. In the EU and UK, Google's consent screen appears before the first ad. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Challenge links.** Sharing a challenge creates a link containing a game seed, your score and a three-letter tag you chose. Nothing else is included.

**Children.** Game Box has no accounts, no sign-up, no chat and no way to upload anything. Before any ad is ever requested, the box asks once, neutrally, for the year you were born. It keeps only an age band (under 13, 13–15, 16+), never the year. For players under 13 every ad request is tagged as child-directed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), so Google serves only general-audience, non-personalised ads and does not build a profile. For players aged 13–15 requests are tagged as under the EU age of consent. A parent can remove ads entirely with the one-time "Go ad-free" purchase, which Apple's Ask to Buy covers.

**Fonts, analytics and recordings.** Game Box uses the system font on your device and loads nothing from third-party font servers. There is no analytics SDK, no session recording and no crash reporter.

**Email.** Game Box never asks for an email address and sends no email.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/nickburum/Mythic-World/issues.
