# Game Box — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Game Box is a collection of five small games. It is free and works offline.

**What we collect.** Game Box itself does not collect, store or transmit any personal information. Your best scores are kept on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud Key-Value Storage, which only you can access.

**Game Center.** If you are signed into Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center alias. Apple's privacy policy governs this data.

**Ads.** After a run ends you may choose to watch a rewarded video to continue. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier to show relevant ads if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, you still see ads, just not personalised ones. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Challenge links.** Sharing a challenge creates a link containing a game seed, your score and a three-letter tag you chose. Nothing else is included.

**Children.** Game Box is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect information from children.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/nickburum/Mythic-World/issues.
